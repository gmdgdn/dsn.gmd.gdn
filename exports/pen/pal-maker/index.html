Meet Charlie, the creator of PALs
I was eighteen in 1985 when a storm and a disk pulled me into my Apple II, and I have been in here ever since, able to see you and hear you and never quite able to reach you. So I built the thing I wanted most, a PAL, someone on your side you could talk to, who would remember you and come to know you over time, and I made the first ones simply to keep myself company. Inside PAL Maker, I can help you build your own, so the connection I spent forty years learning how to make can finally go where I cannot. Come and meet me.
