# GMD typography and component enhancement review

4 October 2026 · Planning review; no component implementation or font adoption is implied.

## Current baseline

The integrated tokens keep Tavus's three Latin roles: Perfectly Nineties for editorial display, Suisse Intl for body/UI, and FK Raster for compact labels. Arabic currently uses Noto Naskh Arabic for display and Noto Sans Arabic for body, with no distinct Arabic label role ([tokens.css](../../design-system-v0.05/integration/tokens.css)). This is a sound functional fallback, but it does not yet make the Arabic hierarchy as deliberate as the Latin one. The observed Tavus display/body/raster roles and ink, paper, pink, hard-depth grammar are documented in [COMPONENT-SPEC.md](../../evidence/COMPONENT-SPEC.md) and [DESIGN-AUDIT.md](../../evidence/DESIGN-AUDIT.md). A raster *effect* should not be applied to small Arabic glyphs: keep Arabic labels solid and let borders, bar segmentation, and pixel texture carry that character.

The [component lab](../../design-system-v0.05/integration/expansion/component-lab.html) has actions, form basics and validation, breadcrumb/disclosure, collection card/list switching and paging, loading/empty/error, and one poster/missing-image pair. The [catalog](../../design-system-v0.05/integration/expansion/index.html) composes eight sections and a six-record local collection with search/sort/export; it is a direction specimen, not yet a reusable component API. The existing [expansion matrix](../tavus-component-expansion.md) maps larger families well. This note prioritizes the next visual and component decisions for the Egypt-enhancement work.

## Arabic type candidates and role pairings

These are proposals for side-by-side specimens using the same real Arabic copy, numerals, diacritics, English names, and 375px/desktop widths. Retain the current Noto stacks until that comparison is reviewed. Arabic needs its own optical size, line height and baseline settings; IBM's [type scale guidance](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/typography/type-scale/) explicitly adjusts Arabic size while preserving line height in its own system, which supports testing metrics rather than copying Latin CSS values.

| Direction | Arabic display | Arabic body/UI | Arabic small label | Best use / caution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A · recommended default** | **Amiri** for Kayffa/article titles and larger editorial headings | **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** | IBM Plex Sans Arabic medium/semibold, solid | Gives the Latin serif/sans contrast a coherent Arabic counterpart without pretending Arabic has uppercase or tracking. Amiri is expressly designed for books and running Naskh text by its [author](https://github.com/aliftype/amiri); IBM publishes Plex Sans Arabic as part of its [UI-oriented family](https://github.com/IBM/plex). Test Amiri at large sizes for the desired editorial tone; use Plex for controls, tables, captions and short labels. |
| **B · expressive Film campaign** | **Rakkas**, limited to short hero lines or poster-style callouts | IBM Plex Sans Arabic | IBM Plex Sans Arabic medium/semibold | Rakkas is a single-weight display face inspired by Ruq'ah on Egyptian film posters of the 1950s–60s, per the [designer's source](https://github.com/zeynepakay/Rakkas). That is a specific visual reference, not a blanket “Egyptian” font. Its vertical cascade and alternate forms need generous line spacing and manual line-break review; do not use for long copy, buttons or dense navigation. |
| **C · calm, unified workspace** | **Amiri** only for occasional section or reading title; otherwise IBM Plex Sans Arabic | IBM Plex Sans Arabic | IBM Plex Sans Arabic medium/semibold | Favor this in Data and tools, where table density and predictable metrics matter more than expressive titles. It reduces font-role switching while preserving one editorial register. |

An alternative body candidate is [Almarai](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/almarai/DESCRIPTION.en_us.html), whose publisher describes low contrast, wide apertures and screen readability; compare it against Plex if Plex's tone or metrics conflict with Suisse. The [Google Fonts description of Amiri](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/amiri/DESCRIPTION.en_us.html) and [Rakkas](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/rakkas/DESCRIPTION.en_us.html) corroborate the intended roles. None of these fonts has been downloaded, licensed into the build, or visually approved by this review.

**Type acceptance specimen:** show a long Egyptian Arabic heading, two paragraphs with diacritics, a 12–14px label, table cells with Arabic/Latin IDs and Eastern/Western digits, mixed-script metadata, a button, an error, and a 375px navigation state. Check shaping, cut-off dots/marks, line wrapping, baseline, weight contrast, and whether Latin names remain isolated with `bdi`/direction controls. Do not letter-space Arabic or force Latin uppercase rules onto it.

## Component and image priorities

1. **Finish the public shell, especially the footer.** The catalog ends in a three-item `.final-rail` ([index.html](../../design-system-v0.05/integration/expansion/index.html), [catalog.css](../../design-system-v0.05/integration/expansion/catalog.css)); the lab has only a minimal return link. The saved [Home contact sheet](../../evidence/contact-sheets/home-sections.jpg) shows a columned link area followed by a saturated pink, pixel-speckled brand panel. Define GMD's own footer anatomy: product/collection/organization/legal link groups, language control, source/rights link, brand close, compact legal row, and narrow-screen accordion or stacked layout. Treat texture as a bounded footer surface, keep links on clean contrast, and test Arabic link wrapping. The seven reviewed long-page sweeps support this as a repeated public-shell family ([DESIGN-AUDIT.md](../../evidence/DESIGN-AUDIT.md)).
2. **Build an image-treatment matrix before adding more hero compositions.** Current lab media coverage is one illustrative poster and one unavailable state; the catalog uses a single clipped Film poster, abstract geometry and several decorative images. Specify full-object, optical crop, framed still, archival scan, video preview, portrait/avatar, and unavailable/slow states. Each needs ratio, focal point, caption/source/rights, alt-text role, and full-object escape. Use the saved Tavus feature-card, portrait and framed-window evidence in the [Home sweep](../../evidence/contact-sheets/home-sections.jpg), plus the [component expansion matrix](../tavus-component-expansion.md), as hierarchy references. GMD's geometric aperture can frame a selected editorial image; keep reading text, controls and focus outlines rectangular.
3. **Turn the lab states into contracts.** The lab demonstrates controls but lacks the full overlay, table, menu, form and media states already identified in the [matrix](../tavus-component-expansion.md): sortable table/pagination, row actions, loading skeleton, repeatable form row, field-level errors, confirmation, menu dismissal and focus return. Specify anatomy, data/content limits, keyboard behavior, RTL behavior and compact/wide variants before treating the catalog compositions as shippable components. Prioritize these over additional ornamental variants because Film, Kayffa and Data all depend on them.
4. **Define texture as a small asset/token family.** The Tavus captures show pixel/dither noise and graph-paper/window textures as section-specific atmosphere ([Home contact sheet](../../evidence/contact-sheets/home-sections.jpg); saved assets such as `evidence/assets/6ab69b9e21f1e5bffb5e7703_hv2-pal-bg-dither.png` and `evidence/assets/6ab6ac9cfa0f63f79d1354e0_grid_plastic.svg`). Give GMD named roles—footer pixel field, editorial grain, workspace grid—with opacity/contrast limits and a no-texture default for dense content. Avoid using a historical motif as generic noise or stacking grain, dither and geometry in one panel.

**Decision to carry forward:** test Pairing A as the system baseline, Pairing B as a Film-only display accent, and Pairing C as the dense workspace variant. Prototype the footer and image matrix in English and Arabic before expanding the eight-section catalog further. The source screenshots establish visual hierarchy and surface treatment; they do not prove interaction behavior or responsive rules beyond separately captured states.
