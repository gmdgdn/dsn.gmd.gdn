# GMD social composition family

The social pass replaces the older generic mockups with native, editable artboards. The shared web identity supplies typography, color and fine rules; social compositions get their own hierarchy and format rules.

## What the brand kit contributes

The supplied Tavus Brand Lite archive contains twenty flat social banner PNGs, ten approximately 4:1 and ten approximately 3:1. Its variants use large negative space, identity contrast, cropped photographs, pixel fields and a single dimensional focal object. The archive contains logo SVGs but no layered social-template source. See `../../../reviews/brand-kit-social-findings.md` from this file's directory, or the review gallery link, for the full inventory.

GMD adopts those composition principles, with its own wordmark, Nile palette and cultural graphics. The original Tavus marks are reference material rather than artwork in GMD exports. Portrait covers, stories and cards are new GMD compositions; those formats were not supplied as editable Tavus templates.

## Composition contract

Choose one dominant element per canvas: title, image or graphic. Use one compact supporting tier. A social card should read at feed scale before the viewer opens it. Avoid miniature navigation, fake video-player controls, dense source tables and a button repeated on every design.

The identity is consistent through type, palette, spacing and image rules. Templates can vary their silhouettes and hierarchy without repeating the same ornament. Use contemporary medallions as editorial graphics, Egyptian floral registers as bands or cover structure, and photographs/posters as media. Keep fine ornamental detail away from body text.

Internal notes—study status, source uncertainty, construction verification and export instructions—belong in the specification and gallery metadata, not the main message on a finished template. Do not invent restoration, custody, launch, audience or distribution metrics to fill a layout.

## Format presets

| Working preset | Canvas | Composition rule |
|---|---|---|
| Identity banner | 1584 × 396 | Large signature, short statement, graphic concentrated in one bay |
| Media banner | 1500 × 500 | Dominant media crop and one concise title |
| Editorial portrait | 1080 × 1350 | Strong title/image sequence; clear cover margin and metadata tier |
| Knowledge/data square | 1080 × 1080 | One idea, diagram or quote; supporting text stays brief |
| Story | 1080 × 1920 | Vertical narrative with generous top/bottom breathing room |
| Link card | 1200 × 630 | Short headline and a recognizable product or visual signature |

These are GMD design presets. They are not a claim of current upload requirements or guaranteed avatar/UI safe areas on a particular social platform. Platform-specific cropping must be checked when a template is used in a campaign.

Use approximately 5–7% canvas margins as a starting point. Story content should reserve generous space around its main message; decorative graphics may intentionally bleed. Distinguish an intentional graphic crop from an accidental text crop. A portrait design must be recomposed for a banner rather than scaled wholesale.

## Type and color

Perfectly Nineties leads English editorial messages; Suisse Intl holds concise supporting copy; FK Raster is limited to short utility/series labels. Arabic compositions use Noto Sans Arabic and a right-aligned, deliberately composed hierarchy. Review punctuation and mixed-direction brand URLs, rather than reversing English content.

Paper/ink/Nile are the shared identity. Pink provides contrast in selected graphic or emphasis zones. Sand and warm grounds support quieter archive material. Preserve adequate contrast, especially for small labels over images. Pixel/dither accents are optional graphic texture, not a required layer on every template.

## Source and editing

The native source is the active Pen document used by the unified system. Individual template node IDs, authored text, dimensions and export paths are recorded in `templates.json`. PNGs are delivery exports, not the editable source. The download bundle contains PNGs plus the registry/specification.

The cinema poster is carried from the existing local review material; source/rights were not independently established. Geometric art is a contemporary source study rather than a claimed facsimile. Keep those notes with the asset registry when replacing media for public use.

## Acceptance

Verify actual PNG dimensions, readable hierarchy at reduced scale, full-resolution type, Arabic shaping, no accidental clipping, no overlapping root boards and correct download links. Compare every layout with the kit's balance of identity, breathing room and focal graphics. Record deliberate bleed/crop exceptions. Refresh the older social board and its linked exports after the individual artboards pass review.
