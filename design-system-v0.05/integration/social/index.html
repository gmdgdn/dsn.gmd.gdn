The reference is a composition language.

Space and identity

Large blank areas and a clear signature replace crowded website-like panels.

One dominant visual

Texture, media or geometry takes the lead. Supporting copy stays brief and legible.

Adapt by format

Recompose banners, covers and stories individually. Keep cultural motifs distinct and functional text outside their detail.

The kit supplies flattened banner references. GMD templates are newly authored native Pen layouts; the vendor's logo and 3D symbol are not part of GMD artwork.
