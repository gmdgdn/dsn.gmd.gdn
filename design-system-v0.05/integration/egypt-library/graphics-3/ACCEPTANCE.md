This is an additive collection. Existing EG/G2 assets, pages and Pen boards remain unchanged. The user's filenames are briefs; historical names, dates and attributions in filenames remain unverified source metadata.

## Orientation decisions

- G3-007 and G3-008: turn the photographed footer strips 90° clockwise; rebuild the running figures and ground line horizontally. Keep the two strips distinct.
- G3-016: turn 90° counterclockwise so the heart point faces down. Retain the many small drawn faces and figures within its contour.
- G3-017: turn 90° counterclockwise so chair backs face up and legs face down. These are chairs, even though their stacked composition resembles a façade.
- G3-014: retain the elongated angular man and the quieter surrounding drawings. Do not substitute a bird or building.
- G3-013: preserve the woman, dress and patterned headdress; make the requested seven-triangle variation explicit.
- G3-021: recreate the Arabic question mark and separate dot, removing interior text.

## Cairo architecture

Two complete visible façade illustrations, independently editable by floor, opening, rail, ornament and roof. Reconstruct the photographed elevation without inventing hidden sides. Retain distinctive proportions, bay counts, unequal rooflines and crowns; do not substitute a generic repeating-window building.

Extract close-up details as separate assets: scrolling iron balcony rail, round baluster rail, cornice/dentil/fluted detail, pointed/trefoil balcony rail and nested pointed opening where readable. Separate bounded details from actual repeating units. Repeatable patterns require a visible seam test.

## Delivery requirements

1. Original source beside each reconstruction, with rotation and simplification notes.
2. SVG geometry with named groups and editable fills/strokes; embedded photographs are not described as editable vector reconstructions.
3. Source image and calligraphic portrait remain intact where exact lettering cannot be reconstructed reliably; no fabricated Arabic lettering.
4. Four complete web compositions with different densities: company mission, Film editorial, Kayffa archive reading, and business data workspace. Include sourced imagery, illustration, navigation, content, meaningful controls and substantial footers.
5. Tavus base: shared paper/ink/Nile tokens, segmented rectangular navigation, striped separators, media windows, hard depth, editorial typography and ruled directory footers. Ornament complements content and does not obscure controls.
6. Pen additions in new named, non-overlapping boards. Review exported proofs before claiming completion.
7. Check SVG appearance, local references, responsive overflow, source orientation and actual control behavior. Preserve prior work and record remaining limitations honestly.
