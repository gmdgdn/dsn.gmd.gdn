COMMON QUESTIONS

A clear scope makes better work.

What information should I bring?

A short description of your material or research question, any current list or data structure, and the people who will use the result.

Does digitization planning mean scanning?

This service page describes metadata and workflow planning for digitization handoffs. It does not imply a scanning facility.

Can a project focus on just one part?

Yes. Collection description, taxonomy, a dataset, or a portal brief can each form a focused scope.
