01THE COMPANY

A SHARED CULTURAL FOUNDATION

Make Egyptian data easier to find, understand and use.

GMD connects Egyptian data with digital experiences that make it easier to discover and use.

We collect, structure and distribute Egyptian data across cultural knowledge, businesses, products and services. By connecting what belongs together, we make it easier for people and organizations to discover and work with that information.

One shared data foundation can support many kinds of access—from research datasets and AI data directions to portals, business tools, films and public knowledge resources.
