# GMD public website — current priority

The user wants the GMD company website first. Earlier workspace, Film and Kayffa interface drafts are preserved but deferred.

## Company story

GMD collects, organizes, segments and distributes Egyptian data and content. It connects cultural collections with research, AI datasets, public discovery, products and business tools. Explain the work directly, without invented scale, clients, launch dates or performance figures.

## Pages

- `gmd/index.html`: company homepage with mission, collection-to-distribution process, product portfolio, data examples, service introduction and contact route.
- `gmd/services.html`: collection and sourcing, metadata/taxonomy, segmentation, distribution, dataset preparation and custom portals/tools. Explain inputs, outputs and how to discuss a project.
- `gmd/contact.html`: inquiry brief with local validation and review. Clearly confirm that no inquiry is sent from this static specimen.

## Portfolio

Film.gmd.gdn, Kayffa Archive, Egyptian Wikipedia, datasets, games, data portals and tools are GMD product areas. Show them within the company portfolio. Link Film to the user-provided domain; other routes should lead to useful company descriptions or contact rather than invented live applications.

## Visual grammar

Retain the Tavus-derived paper, ink, Nile blue, pink accent, hard offset depth, striped section bars, editorial framing and dense ruled footer. Use the user's supplied building SVG and existing images, motifs and patterns; do not create new graphic interpretations. Vary compositions: broad illustrated hero, process rail, asymmetric portfolio, data specimen window, service list and substantial footer. Avoid every section becoming an identical grid.

Arabic headings/interface: Mada. Arabic paragraphs: Amiri. Selective large pixel accents: Handjet. Latin headings/interface: Source Sans 3; paragraphs: Source Serif 4; pixel accents: Handjet. Existing Perfectly Nineties remains an optional large brand display role.

## Completion gate

Review the three public pages at desktop and narrow mobile widths. Check source images, navigation, contact validation/review, visible focus, font roles and any offered Arabic switch. Record partial translation honestly. Save proofs, QA and checkpoint before handoff. New pages remain static prototypes until a real backend is connected.
