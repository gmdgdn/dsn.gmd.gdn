## Recommendation

**Direction A — Polygonal construction system.**

Use Bonner’s polygonal technique as the spatial grammar: a tiling, a contact angle, then outline / fill / band / interlace. Assign families by role, not as wallpaper.

| Product | Lead family | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Film | 4.8² octagon–square (Figs.123–126) | Orthogonal apertures, strong media windows, many skins on one lattice. |
| Kayffa / encyclopedia | Fivefold modules + radial (Figs.187, 258) | Distinct from Film; crop-to-region precedent. |
| Data / knowledge | Dual-level Type A (Fig.448) | Primary path = relation; secondary = texture. Scale 2√3, 90° turn. |
| Covers / campaigns | 12/8 compound (Figs.379–382) | Two star orders, two hierarchy classes. |
| Tools / forms | Same system, strokes hidden | Geometry remains in allocation only. |
| Games | Playful density inside the shared shell | Do not invent a second brand. |

Egyptian booklet families stay a **separate labelled register** (spines, pendants, square modules). Do not blend them into one “heritage” style.

**Why not B or C first.** Direction B (dual-scale fabric everywhere) is the most distinctive knowledge surface, but Fig.448’s exact secondary registration is still a study, and it overpowers tools and long reading. Direction C (register + canopy hybrid) is useful for encyclopedia/Kayffa, but if it leads the whole system the Islamic constructions collapse into decoration again.
