04 · Reconstructions already running

Rules, not traced stars.

Parametric engine in geometry/gmdgeo.py: tiling → rays at contact angle θ → motif segments → strands → bands / interlace. Validated: area ratio 1, no overlaps, no unmatched edges, no unpaired rays, no interior seam breaks, interlace conflicts 0 on the cases below.

05 · Three directions

Same tokens. Different jobs for the geometry.

All three keep paper, ink, Nile, pink, serif / sans / raster, segmented nav, stripe bars, and 5 / 3 / 0 hard depth. They differ in which construction organizes the page.

Direction A · recommended

Polygonal construction system

Recommend

Octagon aperture · 4.8² cell · sample poster, not a rights statement

Film · film.gmd.gdn · existing project

A window cut from the lattice, not a card with a star on it.

The 4.8² cell is the media opening. Neighbouring squares hold title, year, credits, and provenance. The same lattice, at a quieter skin, becomes a collection index. Tools hide the strokes and keep the spans.

Why this direction

Thesis. Every surface starts as a tiling + contact angle. Product themes change family, density, and whether strokes are drawn. Geometry remains in the allocation of space when ornament is off.

Assign. Film → 4.8² (Figs.123–126). Kayffa / encyclopedia → fivefold + radial (Figs.187, 258). Data → dual-level hex (Fig.448). Covers → 12/8 (Fig.379). Tools → same spans, no motif. Games → denser skin inside the shared shell.

Egyptian booklet stays a labelled second family: square modules, pendant registers, spines. Used where a chapter or archive rail needs a different motion. Never mixed into one “heritage” fill.

Tradeoff: less immediately exotic than covering every page in dual-level or radial ornament. Gain: one engine, honest reconstructions, usable forms, portable SVG.

Direction B

Dual-scale knowledge fabric

Ambitious
Selected path · sample relation

Kayffa · existing project

Primary band is the relation. Secondary field is the archive.

Fig.448 logic: identical pattern at two scales, 90° apart. A selected Nile band would be a real edge in the graph, not a highlight wash.

Thesis. Dual-level Type A is the system. Macro geometry = navigation and selected paths. Micro geometry = collection texture. Fig.453 (mixed families, scale ~1:13.7) reserved for rare covers — and only if reconstructed from its modules, not faked with nested stars.

Tradeoff: the most distinctive knowledge surface, and the hardest to keep faithful. Secondary registration is not certified yet. Overpowers tools, tables, and long reading if used as a default field.

Register, spine, and canopy

سجل أفقي، وعمود فهرسة، وفتحة دائرية للنص.

العائلات المصرية في الكتيّب (المربعات، السجلات المعلقة، المراوح الشعاعية) تُبنى كعائلة مستقلة: إيقاع رأسي وأفقي، لا نجمة إسلامية ملصقة على الخلفية. النص العربي يعاد ترتيب المكوّنات فيه، ولا تُعكس أشكال البناء.

Thesis. Lead with Egyptian-booklet registers and radial canopies (PDF pp.8–16, 20–27) plus Bonner’s gore (Fig.490) for segmented headers. Islamic lattices become optional graphic fields, not the spatial engine.

Tradeoff: strong for encyclopedia and archive spines, and this RTL block shows why registers matter. If C leads the whole ecosystem, Film and Data lose the construction system we just proved we can build. Treat C as a composition family inside A, not as the master direction.

06 · Recommendation

Build Direction A. Keep B and C as compositions inside it.

A leads

One construction engine. Product themes pick a family and a density. Quiet utility is the same grammar with strokes off. Matches the brief: geometry organizes space.

B is a theme

Use dual-level for Kayffa / data atlas compositions after Fig.448 registration is overlaid on the plate. Do not make it the default skin.

C is a composition

Register / spine / canopy become layout families 4 and 5. Egyptian sources stay attributed and unblended.

07 · After you review

Implementation sequence.

  1. You confirm Direction A, or name the change (B-led, C-led, or a hybrid rule).
  2. Stage 2. Eight families: 4.8² (already in), 4.6.12, Fourfold A subset, fivefold modules, 12/8 plate overlay, Fig.448 overlay, radial/gore, bands/interlace. Each: construction diagram, cell, repeat, field, ±, outline/fill/band/interlace, source-faithful vs labelled GMD adaptation, SVG, parameters.
  3. Stage 3. Tokens: visual + geometric (family, pitch, θ, density, aperture, joint, crop, optical tiers). Macro / medium / micro + minimum sizes.
  4. Stage 4. Six compositions: editorial aperture, collection lattice, knowledge atlas, radial canopy, register/spine, quiet utility. Long content, missing image, narrow, RTL.
  5. Stage 5. Component library with the listed states. Geometry on frames, not on hit targets or tables.
  6. Stage 6–7. Six product themes. Four web studies: ecosystem, Film or Kayffa, data/archive, tools or encyclopedia. Desktop, narrow, one real Arabic/RTL composition.
  7. Copy v0.01 into a new v0.05 Pen file only if Pen is available. Until then, HTML/SVG masters in this folder. v0.01 stays intact.
