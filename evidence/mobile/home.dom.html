Our Research
Best-in-class TURN-TAKING

Sparrow [2]

Sparrow is the #1 conversational understanding model on the market, built to understand not just what someone says, but when to listen, wait, speak, and respond. It understands the entire audio scene - including pauses, interruptions, backchannels, multiple speakers, and background noise - to make AI conversations feel more naturally human.
#1 on TurnBench - best conversational turn-taking model on market
Lowest error rate - fewer missed turns, false interruptions, and awkward conversational mistakes
Best-in-class rendering

Phoenix [4.5]

Phoenix is the most natural and realistic human rendering model on the market, designed to generate lifelike faces, expressions, gestures, and upper-body movement in real time.  It can render entirely new identities zero-shot from a single image, making photorealistic conversational AI faster and easier to create than ever before.
134ms latency - fastest in market, 25% faster than any other model
Preferred 2:1 - in blind head-to-head testing against nearest competitor
Best-in-class perception

Raven [1]

Raven is a new type of multi-modal perception model, giving machines the ability to see, hear and understand in real-time. It translates facial expressions, tone, gaze, emotion, and environmental context into rich conversational signals, helping your AI respond with empathy, awareness, and intent.
3 simultaneous inputs - understands voice, video, and screen context together
<300ms context freshness - not a snapshot, a continuously rolling read of emotional, environmental and attentional state

Best-in-class interaction

Griffin [Preview]

Griffin is the first human interaction model: a video-to-video model that sees, hears, speaks and moves at the same time, like a person. It keeps listening while it talks, responds to what you show it and gestures as it speaks, so for the first time a machine can hold up its end of a face-to-face conversation.

48% thought it was a real person - on live video, making it the first model to pass the Turing test face to face
#1 on NVIDIA VideoFDB - top score on both perception and generation in NVIDIA's benchmark for face-to-face AI
