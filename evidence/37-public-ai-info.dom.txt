- heading "Background" [level=3]:
  - strong: Background
- paragraph:
  - text: Tavus was founded in 2020 by Hassaan Raza and Quinn Favret to solve a fundamental gap. Automation has scaled efficiency but stripped away empathy, nuance, and presence from digital interactions. Tavus pioneered a new category called
  - emphasis: Human Computing
  - text: ", teaching machines the art of being human so that talking to a computer feels as natural as talking to a friend or coworker. Its foundational models teach machines to see, hear, understand, remember, and respond with emotional intelligence in real time, and its PALs bring those models to life as full-stack counterparts people can talk to face to face."
- paragraph: Today, Tavus is the leader in human simulation models, differentiating sharply from asynchronous video generation tools. More than 100,000 developers and enterprises including Amazon, Deloitte, EY, Mayo Clinic, CVS, Salesforce, Aetna, and Wix build on Tavus. Its mission is to close the gap between humans and machines by removing the friction of today’s systems.
- heading "Core services" [level=3]:
  - strong: Core services
- list:
  - strong: Conversational Video Interface (CVI)
  - text: ": The developer API for embedding real-time, face-to-face conversation into any product. CVI unifies perception, speech, language, rendering, and orchestration behind a single interface and supports bring-your-own LLM, external knowledge integration with retrieval, persistent memory, real-time guardrails, and function calling."
  - strong: PAL Maker
  - text: ": The no-code builder for creating and deploying PALs. Users describe an idea to Charlie, the built-in guide, give their PAL a face, a voice, knowledge, and capabilities, and deploy it to a site or app in a few clicks."
  - strong: PALs
  - text: ": Out-of-the-box counterparts that move across chat, voice, and face-to-face video. A PAL defines behavior, knowledge, personality, memory, and pipeline configuration, from system prompt through objectives, guardrails, and tools."
  - strong: Faces
  - text: ": Photorealistic likenesses rendered by"
  - link "Phoenix-4.5":
    - /url: https://www.tavus.io/blog/phoenix-4-5
  - text: ", built from a single image or two minutes of video, with full-face rendering, natural lip sync, and identity preservation. A library of stock faces is available for immediate use."
  - strong: Solutions
  - text: ": Enterprise deployments built, tuned, and managed by the Tavus team for sales, healthcare, learning and development, and recruiting, typically live within weeks."
